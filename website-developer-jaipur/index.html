Who it suits

What that can look like for a business in Jaipur

Examples of the problems a website or web app solves, not a list of past clients. If yours is different, describe it — the conversation costs nothing.

A shop, showroom or clinic people search for

Customers look you up before they visit. A site with your services, timings, location and a one-tap WhatsApp or call button turns that search into a visit or an enquiry, instead of sending them to whoever has a better page.

An exporter, manufacturer or trader with overseas buyers

Buyers abroad judge you by your website before they reply to an email. A fast, credible English-language catalogue site with a clear enquiry route does that job without a salesperson in their time zone.

A coaching institute, school or training business

Admissions enquiries, batches, students and fees tracked in one place — a web app with logins for staff and an admin dashboard, rather than registers and spreadsheets.

A startup that needs its first working product

Accounts, a dashboard, an API and a database, built so the second version does not mean starting again — by one developer accountable from the database to the deployed site.

What I do not do I am one developer, not an agency. I do not take on logo and brand identity work, ad campaigns or monthly SEO retainers. Every site I build ships with clean page titles, descriptions and heading structure, but if you need ongoing marketing, I will tell you that rather than sell it.

FAQ

Questions Jaipur businesses ask first

How much does a website cost in Jaipur?

With me, a straightforward business website starts at ₹7,000, an admin dashboard at ₹12,000, an AI-powered application at ₹15,000 and a custom web application with logins and a database at ₹20,000. Those are starting points for a small build; the fixed price depends on the number of pages or screens, logins, integrations and who writes the content. You get the figure in writing before any work starts.

Do you only work with businesses in Jaipur?

No. I am based in Jaipur and work with businesses here, but most projects run remotely over WhatsApp, calls and email, so I also work with clients across India and internationally. For a Jaipur business the main difference is that we share a city and a time zone.

Will I own the website and its code?

Yes. You get the source code at handover, and the domain and hosting should be registered in your name, on your account, with you holding the login. I can set those up with you, but they stay yours.

Can you redesign or fix my existing website?

Yes. Performance work, responsive fixes for phones, interface clean-up and new features on a site that already exists. Redesign and maintenance are quoted on scope, because the honest figure depends on what is already there.

Do you build WordPress websites?

My main work is custom websites and React and MERN applications. If WordPress or a page builder is genuinely the better fit for your budget and how often your content changes, I will say so, even when that means you do not need me. React vs WordPress walks through that decision.

How quickly do you reply to an enquiry?

Usually within 24 hours, with a plan, a timeline and a fixed estimate. WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach me. If you are comparing developers first, this guide to choosing a freelance web developer lists the questions worth asking.
